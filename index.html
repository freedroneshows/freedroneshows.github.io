How it works

Your crowd. Their brand. Our fleet.

Underwriting works the way public radio has always worked. A sponsor pays for the program, the audience gets it free, and everyone hears who made it possible.

STEP 01

The venue applies

Tell us about your space, your crowd and your calendar. We check sightlines, launch area and airspace, then confirm the venue qualifies.

STEP 02

A brand underwrites

We match your residency with a sponsor that wants your audience. The sponsor covers production and gets category exclusivity in your sky.

STEP 03

Drone Spectacular Residency flies the season

We handle choreography, permits, insurance, crew and weather calls. After the season, both sides get a recap with attendance, QR scans and social reach.

Safety, airspace & permits

We handle the hard part

Airspace approvals

FAA Part 107 waivers and airspace authorizations in the US, CASA approvals in Australia.

Local permits

We work with the city, fire marshal and venue operations on every show night.

Weather calls

Wind and rain limits are set in advance. If a show can't fly safely, it doesn't, and it gets rescheduled.

Fully insured

Liability coverage on every show, with the venue named as additional insured.

Questions

The fine print, in plain words

For venues

Is it really free?

Yes. The underwriter pays for production. The venue commits to promoting the shows, sharing attendance and hosting the sponsor's on-site activation.

Who picks the sponsor?

Drone Spectacular Residency brings the sponsor and you approve it. You can also introduce a brand you already work with.

Can we say no to a brand?

Yes. Nothing is signed until the venue approves the sponsor and the creative.

What do we need on site?

A secure launch area, a clear audience zone and safe sightlines. We assess all of it before you're accepted.

For brands

What's guaranteed?

The number of shows, your formations and QR moments, and category exclusivity. Weather-scrubbed shows are rescheduled.

How is attendance verified?

Venues report show-night attendance as a condition of the residency, and every QR scan is tracked.

Can we bring our own creative?

Yes. Our designers turn your logo and campaign into formations, and you approve every frame before it flies.

Can we buy more than one venue?

Yes. The Network tier puts one brand across several residencies in a market.
